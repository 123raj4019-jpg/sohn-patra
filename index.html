A Ritual of Gold

Not a product — a ceremony.

Sohnpatra was born from a singular conviction: that the most profound gifting is sensory, emotional and deeply ancestral — drawn from India’s most ancient traditions of offering gold not as ornament, but as nourishment.

24K Pure Edible Gold

The gold you can taste.

For centuries, edible gold has symbolised royalty and prosperity across cultures. At Sohn Patra, every date is finished in certified 24K edible gold — a simple ritual of gifting, transformed into a statement of refinement.

The Connoisseur’s Edit

Twelve creations. One founder’s edit.

A refined curation personally curated by the founder — premium Medjool dates, sophisticated nut blends and globally inspired flavours, each enveloped in silky white, milk and dark chocolate.
